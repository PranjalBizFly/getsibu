==== ====
Features
Everything your team needs, none of the bloat.
A focused toolkit for ingesting, organising and shipping creative work — designed to disappear into your workflow.
[svg]
Auto-ingest from anywhere
Connect Google Drive, local NAS, or drag & drop. Watch folders are scanned continuously.
[svg]
AI-assisted tagging
Suggested tags and scene detection on import. Approve in bulk, override anytime.
[svg]
Search by anything
Full-text, EXIF, OCR, duration, format, uploader, tags. Saved searches share by URL.
[svg]
Comments in context
Threaded discussions on every asset — even at specific timecodes in video.
[svg]
Taxonomies that scale
Hierarchical tags with synonyms, colour, and inheritance. Bulk re-tag in a click.
[svg]
Multi-tenant by design
Each organisation isolated end-to-end. Per-tenant keys, scoped queries, audit logs.
[svg]
Insightful analytics
Storage trends, hot tags, top contributors, video minutes — at the click of a tab.
[svg]
Granular permissions
Owner, admin, manager, editor, individual — with folder-level overrides where you need them.
[svg]
Built for speed
Sub-second search across millions of assets. Streaming previews. Resumable uploads.
