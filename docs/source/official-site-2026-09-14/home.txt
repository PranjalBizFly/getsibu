Q: Can I self-host GetSibu?
A: Business and Enterprise plans include a self-host option. We ship a Docker Compose bundle (API + MongoDB + Redis + worker) and an OpenAPI spec — the same code we run in the cloud.
Q: How does billing work?
A: Monthly or annual on Stripe. Cancel anytime; data stays read-only for 30 days after cancellation in case you change your mind. Invoices and DPAs available on every plan.
Q: Is AI an extra add-on?
A: No. AI tagging, transcription and semantic search are included in Pro and above. You bring your own OpenAI key on the Pro plan; Business+ comes with an included managed pool.
Q: What are the upload limits?
A: Up to 10 GB per file out of the box; configurable in Settings → Storage. Chunked uploads with resume. No monthly upload cap.
Q: Do you support SSO / SCIM?
A: SAML 2.0 and OIDC SSO on Business+. SCIM 2.0 user provisioning on Enterprise. Force-MFA enforcement is per-org and server-side.
Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Choose US, EU, India or AP-Southeast at workspace creation. Storage is encrypted at rest and in transit. GetSibu never trains on your data.
Q: Can I integrate with Slack / Premiere / Frame.io?
A: Yes. Out-of-the-box for Slack and Webhooks; Premiere/AE/Frame.io via our extensions and Zapier. Custom integrations via webhooks + REST API.
Q: What’s the difference between Individual and Organisation?
A: Individual is one private workspace, no roles. Organisation gives you Owner / Admin / Manager / User roles, team management, folder permissions and approval workflows.
