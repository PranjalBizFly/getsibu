About
We build tools that let creative teams find anything, fast
.
GetSibu started inside a media studio drowning in 50 TB of unsorted footage. We built the thing we wished existed — and made it work for everyone else.
==== ====
[IMG src=https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1522071820081-009f0129c71c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=70 alt=GetSibu team]
Our values
Calm software
The right answer should be one click away. Everything else gets out of the way.
Tenant trust
Your data is your data. We design for isolation first and convenience second.
Real workflows
We build with creative teams, not against them. Every feature exits a real pain.
