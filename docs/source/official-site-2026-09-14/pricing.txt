Frequently asked
Can I switch plans later? +
Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime. Proration is handled automatically.
Do you offer a free trial on paid plans? +
Pro includes a 3-day trial, no credit card required.
What happens if I exceed my storage quota? +
Uploads pause until you delete content or upgrade. Existing assets remain accessible.
Is my data isolated from other tenants? +
Yes. Every organisation has scoped queries and per-tenant encryption keys. Audit logs record every cross-tenant access attempt.
Do you support custom contracts and SSO? +
Business and Enterprise plans include SSO, SCIM, custom DPAs, and annual contracts.
