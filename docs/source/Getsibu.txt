1. GetSibu -- Creative Asset Management Platform

GetSibu is an intelligent creative asset management platform designed to help teams store,
organise, search, review and collaborate on videos, images, documents and audio from one
central workspace. Instead of depending on scattered folders, disconnected drives and
complicated naming systems, GetSibu creates a searchable creative library where teams can
quickly locate the right asset and understand its context.

2. The Operating System for Creative Libraries

GetSibu acts as an operating system for modern creative libraries by bringing storage,
organisation, AI tagging, search, collaboration, permissions and analytics into one platform.
Creative teams can connect existing storage sources, import assets, automatically process
media and make the resulting library available to authorised team members.

3. AI-Powered Digital Asset Management

GetSibu combines digital asset management with artificial intelligence to make large creative
libraries easier to understand. AI-assisted tagging, scene detection, OCR, semantic search,
face grouping and duplicate detection help teams organise information that would otherwise
require extensive manual work.

4. Creative Library Management

A growing creative team can quickly accumulate thousands of videos, images, documents and
audio files. GetSibu provides a structured environment where those assets can be organised
through folders, tags, collections, metadata and permissions, creating a reliable source of truth
for the entire organisation.

5. Intelligent Media Library

GetSibu transforms a conventional media library into an intelligent information system. Instead
of searching only by filenames, users can search using descriptions, visual characteristics,
extracted text, tags, people, colours, scenes, formats and other available metadata.

6. Centralised Creative Asset Library

GetSibu provides one central location for creative assets from connected drives, local
infrastructure and direct uploads. By bringing assets together, teams can reduce the confusion
caused by multiple storage locations and disconnected versions of the same file.

7. Modern Digital Asset Management
Traditional file storage focuses primarily on keeping files available. GetSibu extends that
concept by adding AI-assisted organisation, contextual search, collaboration, approvals, version
history, analytics and permission controls specifically designed for creative workflows.

8. Creative Asset Organisation

GetSibu helps teams organise creative assets using structured folders and scalable
taxonomies. Hierarchical tags, synonyms, colours and inheritance make it possible to create an
organisation system that remains useful even when the library grows to thousands or millions of
assets.

9. AI-Aware Asset Management

AI-aware asset management allows GetSibu to analyse media and recommend meaningful tags
and classifications. Teams can approve AI suggestions in bulk, override them when necessary
and maintain control over how their library is organised.

10. One Source of Truth for Creative Teams

GetSibu gives creative, marketing, production and brand teams a shared source of truth for their
digital assets. Everyone works from the same organised library, while permissions and approval
workflows help ensure that users access and distribute the right content.

and distributing digital content. GetSibu extends traditional DAM with AI-assisted tagging,
semantic search and collaboration.

362. What Is AI Asset Management?

AI asset management uses machine intelligence to analyse and classify digital content, making
large libraries easier to search and organise.

363. What Is Semantic Search?

Semantic search allows users to search by meaning and context rather than relying only on
exact keyword matches.

364. What Is AI Tagging?

AI tagging automatically suggests labels based on what the system detects in an asset.

365. What Is Scene Detection?

Scene detection identifies distinct sections or scenes within video content so users can navigate
large files more efficiently.

366. What Is OCR in DAM?

OCR converts text visible inside images and documents into searchable information.

367. What Is Face Grouping?

Face grouping identifies recurring people across media collections and groups related assets
together.
368. What Is Duplicate Detection?

Duplicate detection identifies files that are identical or visually equivalent, including some
renamed or re-encoded copies.

369. What Is Asset Versioning?

Asset versioning maintains previous versions of a file so teams can understand its history and
restore earlier versions when necessary.

370. What Is Creative Approval?

Creative approval is a workflow where authorised users review assets and confirm that content
is ready for use.

371. What Is Creative Governance?

Creative governance establishes rules around asset ownership, permissions, approvals,
retention and usage.

372. What Is Multi-Tenant DAM?

Multi-tenant DAM allows one platform to support multiple isolated organisations or clients.

373. What Is a Creative Library?

A creative library is a central repository for images, videos, documents, audio and other digital
content.

374. What Is Asset Metadata?

Asset metadata is information describing a file, including creator, format, dimensions, keywords
and other contextual details.

375. What Is Asset Taxonomy?

An asset taxonomy is a structured classification system that defines how digital content is
grouped and labelled.

376. What Is a DAM Migration?
